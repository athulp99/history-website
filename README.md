# History of Earth

An interactive website that lets people explore Earth’s 4.54-billion-year story, from planetary formation to the present day.

## Vision

The timeline will turn immense spans of geological and biological time into a clear, explorable experience. Visitors will be able to zoom from eons to individual events, filter by theme, and select milestones for concise context.

## Planned experience

- A responsive, zoomable timeline spanning Earth’s formation to today
- Era and theme filters, including geology, climate, life, extinction events, and human history
- Event cards with dates, descriptions, and source links
- Accessible keyboard navigation and readable, high-contrast controls

## Initial milestones

- 4.54 billion years ago — Earth forms
- 3.5 billion years ago — earliest widely accepted evidence of life
- 541 million years ago — Cambrian Period begins
- 66 million years ago — non-avian dinosaurs become extinct
- Present day — the Holocene and human history

## Status

This repository is at the project-foundation stage. The first implementation will establish the timeline interface and a small, well-sourced dataset.
